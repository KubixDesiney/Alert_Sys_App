tool. It is delivered as part of a dedicated, KubixDesiney-operated instance

industrial alerts, and through which you may use optional features such as
location-based assignment, voice claim, and the Kubix Copilot chat assistant.

## 5. Optional features: location and voice

Some App features are optional and must be enabled by your organization
before you can use them:

- **Location tracking** — when enabled, the App shares your device's GPS
  location for proximity-based assignment and the in-app plant locator. It is
  used only while the feature is active and only within the scope your
  organization configures.
- **Voice claim and voiceprint enrollment** — when enabled, the App can
  capture a voice sample to create a voiceprint used for on-device speaker
  verification (for example, to confirm it is really you claiming an alert
  from a lock screen). Enrollment is optional and, where required by
  applicable law, subject to your consent. See `PRIVACY.md` for how this data
  is handled.

## 6. AI features and industrial-safety disclaimer

The App includes AI-assisted features: automatic assignment suggestions,
predictive risk forecasting, morning briefings, and the Kubix Copilot chat
assistant. **These are decision-support tools, not safety instrumentation.**
SIAS does not monitor, control, or actuate any machinery, and no AI output
(an assignment, a prediction, a forecast, or a Kubix Copilot reply) should be
treated as a safety determination or as a substitute for your organization's
certified safety systems, alarms, and emergency procedures. AI outputs may be
incomplete, delayed, or incorrect. You remain responsible for exercising your
own professional judgment and for following your employer's safety
procedures at all times.

## 7. Kubix Copilot

Kubix Copilot is an AI chat assistant made available to you at `/copilot`
with context about your organization's instance (tenant, company, your name,
and plan). Messages you send may be logged and may, in some cases, result in
KubixDesiney escalating your conversation to a human engineer, in which case
the App will tell you so and a human may follow up by email. Do not enter
data in the chat that you are not authorized to share, and do not rely on
Kubix Copilot's replies for safety-critical decisions (see Section 6).
